Friendly Neighbour Assistant

Friendly Neighbour is a personal assistant used by a single person — its owner — through Telegram. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With the owner's consent it reads the owner's own Google Calendar events, Gmail messages and Google Drive documents in read-only mode, to answer the owner's questions and prepare short daily summaries for the owner. It never sends, edits or deletes anything in the owner's Google account.
